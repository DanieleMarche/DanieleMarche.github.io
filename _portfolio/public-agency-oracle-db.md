**Sopra Steria Italia** · Data Engineer · Public administration · 2025 – 2026

A national government agency needed a new system to manage the **reuse and transfer of its technology assets**. I was responsible for the database behind it.

### What I did

- I started from **loosely defined functional requirements** and turned them into a **conceptual model**, then a **physical model**, and then the implementation in **Oracle**.
- Once live, the system should serve up to **tens of thousands of external users** plus the agency's own staff.

My Bachelor's thesis at Sapienza, on conceptual modelling for data warehousing, was based on my internship at Sopra Steria.
