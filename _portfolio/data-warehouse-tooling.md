**Sopra Steria Italia** · Data Engineer · Public administration · 2025 – 2026

After my internship, I kept working on the data warehouses of public-administration clients. Part of the work was modelling, and part was writing tools to automate the repetitive tasks.

### Dimensional modelling

- I maintained the **dimensional-model repository** of a data warehouse: I updated existing **star schemas** and added new ones.
- My work on the tax-court dashboards in Qlik Sense, from the internship, has [its own page](/portfolio/tax-court-reporting-qlik/).

### Python tooling

- **SAP BusinessObjects universe analyser:** Python scripts that parse universe exports and match objects across universes, to find overlaps and universes worth merging or optimising. They **replaced hours of manual review**.
- **Change-specification generator:** I built the first module of a Python generator for data-warehouse change-specification documents. It saves several hours per document.
